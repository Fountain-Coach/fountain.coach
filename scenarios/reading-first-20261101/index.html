On Continuity

Fountain Coach began with a question about continuity. What remains of a conversation when the people who have shared it are no longer together? What becomes of an observation, a metaphor, a thought that has not yet found its form? And how might technology help preserve these things without claiming to understand them on our behalf?

The original work grew out of coaching and psychological reflection. FountainAI was conceived as a means of keeping the threads of an unfolding conversation available across time. Not as a synthetic therapist, not as an authority dispensing advice, but as an instrument of attention and memory. Its concern was less with answers than with the circumstances in which meaningful questions might return.

Over time, that concern expanded beyond coaching. Dramatic writing, theatrical performance, musical composition and the development of computational instruments brought another question into focus: how does a work retain its identity while passing through different forms?

A written scene may become a spoken reading, a score, a performance or an interactive experience. A book may acquire new chapters, different reading sequences and relationships to other works. Yet its sources, authorship and history must remain recognizable. Change should not require forgetting.

Reframe emerged from this work. It is a reading and writing environment in which books, dramatic texts, sounds, scores and computational instruments can belong to one authored composition. Its underlying systems preserve sources, relationships, revisions and evidence. The purpose is not to make the machinery visible everywhere. It is to make the work available without losing the conditions under which it came into being.

The name Fountain Coach has remained. The domain still ends in .coach, a reminder of its beginnings. The present work is not primarily a coaching service. What survives from that beginning is the conviction that accompaniment need not mean intervention, and that an instrument can support human attention without claiming human judgment.

This publication brings together the literary and artistic work of Benedikt Eickhoff and the continuing development of Fountain Coach. The essays are written as an open, evolving book. Other works retain their own identities, editions, histories and contributors. Some can be read, some heard, some performed. The technological work is documented alongside them, but does not replace them.

Fountain Coach is the publisher. Reframe provides the reading and writing environment. The works belong to their authors.

The invitation is simply to read.
