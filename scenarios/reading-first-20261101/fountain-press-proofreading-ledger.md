## Local spelling and editorial-context verification

**Native macOS language/spelling check completed:** 41 manuscript/prologue source projections were examined with on-device Apple `NSSpellChecker` and `NLLanguageRecognizer` (no external language API). Exact per-work candidate counts and flags are retained locally in `fountain-press-native-spelling-audit.json`. Recognition of mixed German/English works misclassified two predominantly English texts after opening with German titles; these flags are unreliable and **must not be automatically applied**. Most flagged tokens are intentional proper names, UK spellings (`behaviours`, `licence`, `recognise`), German terms, and instrument names (Csound, SatzfunkPlayer).

- **`Antitude`** in *I Keep Moving* is explicitly described in the following sentence as a meaningful invented word; intentional, **do not correct**.
- **`The Teatre of the Mind`** retains the actual authored source title. Whether `Teatre` is deliberate remains an **editorial decision**; do not silently rename the work or rewrite its permanent source address.
- **`The Ear That Learns`** includes several original website controls and evidence-link labels as printed prose near the conclusion (`the MIDI 2.0 score`, `Ear's measured comparison`, `teaching-traditions contract`, and `Open the original published work`). These are **interface residue / source-presentation candidates** that should be separated into a correctly attributed addendum, never surgically erased from the manuscript without the author's decision and original-source comparison.
- **`The Door Is a Score`** carefully distinguishes the three actual CC0 sound files from speculative automatic narrator, MIDI2 or complete semantic engine integration. Future-tense technological claims are historical/proposed, not evidence of an active capability; check source-date and current release when publishing new editions.
- **`Reframe and the Business Value of the Estate`** uses `estate` as the central historical/business thesis; the injunction against the public `Estate` *navigation label* does not license a global search-and-replace through the writer's prose.
- **23 / 30 active First Reading works:** gallery principal art exists but the authored reading-body template contains no inline figure. This requires source, rights, and placement review per work; the reader cannot automatically annex gallery art to the text.
- **First Readings collection cover:** the `img` in the decorative collection cover reports empty `alt` and no explicit source dimensions in this token-level inventory. This **does not prove an accessibility error**: the owning link/visible caption and decorative-image semantics must be judged from the actual accessibility tree; no fabricated `alt` inserted.
- **References and factual claims:** exact historical, technological, source/licensing and present-capability statements were not independently reverified across all 35 current works. They remain editorial evidence checks rather than certified factual findings. Audio/source-word alignment and external-link availability also require their own witnesses.

**No manuscript prose was revised.** Editorial correction is a separate, source-bound authoring act. This ledger records which works were machine-checked, where real layout/source problems were reproduced, and which items require human editorial adoption. It is **not** a claim of comprehensive linguistic or factual proof with no remaining issues.
