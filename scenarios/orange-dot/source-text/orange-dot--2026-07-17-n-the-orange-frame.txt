I don’t believe the big room. It looks too ready to be remembered. The yellow wall says it was important, the orange frame says something happened there, the papers on the floor say people left in a hurry, but they all agree too much. I would start with the chair and draw around it until the room stops helping. No, not the chair—the space under it. Then the yellow can go. The frame can stay for one line because it cannot decide whether it is a cage or a shelf. The little platform is wrong because it already knows where the child belongs. I would move it halfway out of the room. The score thinks false memory means that something was invented. I think it is when everything fits together so well that nobody notices the part that does not.
