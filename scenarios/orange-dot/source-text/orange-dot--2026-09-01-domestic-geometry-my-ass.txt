Domestic geometry my ass.

I schnippel die drei Surfer erstmal auseinander because together they already look like a brochure. Blue board left, yellow board too high, black one almost disappearing. Better. One of them gets only feet. Another keeps the shoulders. Der dritte kriegt gar keinen Kopf because enough personality already.

Then the bag people. One standing, one sitting, both mit Zeug. I cut the bags bigger. Much bigger. Sorry, you are your luggage now. The standing one gets moved too close to the seated one, almost touching, which makes the whole thing more embarrassing and therefore useful.

The hanging bag I love. Completely absurd. I put it above the corridor like weather. It can fall or not fall. Nobody knows. The corridor itself gets shortened because ten meters of depth is too much authority. Schnippel schnippel, now it is just a dark mouth with one tiny chair-person inside. Cute. Horrible. Good.

The yellow room gets all the vessels removed except one tiny fat orange thing. The man can stay because he looks like he has no idea why he was invited. Same.

And the glass cube with the drying rack — nein. Too perfect. I cut one corner open. The rack leans out. Domestic geometry my ass.

Then I take the little diagrams, the circles and squares and arrows, and mix them into the scenes where they don’t belong. One focus cross on a surfboard. One stage direction under a bag. One ground-field dot inside the corridor.

Now the score stops behaving.

Much better.
