DRIFT NOTES — not a story yet.

I’m writing from the part of the day that doesn’t want interpretation. The body asks for certainty; the page offers a box. So I give it boxes. Clean edges. Measured distance. No confessions turned into plot.

I’m in log mode.

01 — Room.
A cube with a door cut into it. The first fact is always architecture: there is an inside, and there is a way out. I stand at the threshold long enough to notice the air. Nothing dramatic. Just the truth that a room holds what it holds, even when no one explains it.

02 — Chair.
A seat with its patience. It doesn’t call me. It doesn’t judge me. It waits like a tool waits. I don’t sit yet. I record that sitting exists.

03 — Animal.
A black shape on the floor of the grid—heavy, stubborn, calm in its own mass. I don’t name it. Naming makes it symbolic too quickly. I only note: there is something in me that refuses to be translated. It eats, it stands, it insists.

04 — Loop.
A simple circular arrow. The day repeats. The mind repeats. The same thought returns wearing different coats. Reset is not failure. Reset is the only motion I can trust when everything else drifts.

05 — Bundle.
Sticks gathered and tied. Evidence of a hand that did something small on purpose. Not a solution—just a binding. A temporary “keep.” A way to say: this won’t scatter today.

Next stable frame: a blank screen.
Not absence—projection space. The place where the story could show itself, if it earns it. I don’t force it. I don’t chase it. I leave the screen empty the way winter leaves streets empty: not to punish, but to make the next footprint visible.

Indicator: ON.
A small orange dot like a pilot light. Not romance, not panic—just confirmation that the system is awake.

Approved for uncertainty.
That stamp makes me almost laugh. Bureaucracy for the soul. A permit to not resolve.

I repeat the rule because it’s the only kindness that scales:
Don’t solve. Record.

Tonight I don’t become a hero or a prophet. I become the clerk of the quiet. I inventory what still has shape. I let the rest drift until it finds its own container.
