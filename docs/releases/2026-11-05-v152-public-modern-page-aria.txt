        - heading "Benedikt Eickhoff" [level=2]
        - paragraph: Before a printer chooses a face or an alignment, a more fundamental arrangement has begun. There is the Format, the outer dimension of the sheet. Within it lies the Satzspiegel—the type area in which words and images will be composed. Between that area and the cut edges are the Stege, the margins. The Zeilenfall determines where lines end; Durchschuss, the leading, gives them breathing room.
        - paragraph: The useful question is not which page looks more up to date. It is which decisions its spaces make visible—and which activities those spaces permit.
        - paragraph: A page can look new and repeat an old decision. It can also look entirely conventional while allowing something genuinely new to happen.
        - paragraph: "The next two settings form a small demonstration within the page: two distinctly framed type specimens of one passage. Their words are deliberately identical; their composition changes."
