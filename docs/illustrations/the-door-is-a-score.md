# The Door Is a Score — opening artwork

Authored selection for the essay opening, gallery card and Open Graph/Twitter image. Generated with the built-in OpenAI image tool, 27 October 2026; 1774 × 887 PNG. This is an interpretive Teatro graphic score, not measured audio data, an executable Scenario, or an archival image. The measured Csound score remains in the listening study.

Direction: original contemporary experimental graphic score; flat black spatial masses, burnt-orange impacts, one cool blue accent, fresh off-white ground. A sharp empty threshold separates planes. Preparation, distance, recurrence and withheld action are represented through spatial relations and silence. Small sans-serif title and TEATRO signature. Asymmetric, materially vivid, rigorous negative space. No parchment, classical serif title, literal architectural engraving, or imitation of an existing artwork.

The author rejected the initial period-looking illustration and requested that the estate and his Facebook stream be consulted. The logged-in Safari view informed the revised graphic direction. No Facebook image is reproduced or incorporated.
